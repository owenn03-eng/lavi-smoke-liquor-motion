# Why this motion pass should convert better

Owen can use these bullets when comparing **original** → **static redesign** → **motion pass**.

1. **Original feels like a brochure builder site** (emoji, multi-page IA, no age ritual). Motion pass keeps the redesign’s premium dual-business story but makes the homepage *behave* like a flagship product site — people stay long enough to hit Call / Directions.

2. **Static redesign already fixed hierarchy and photography**; this pass adds scroll-driven pacing so “What we stock” and “818” aren’t flat cards — they’re a journey that ends in a phone number, not a fake cart.

3. **Age-gate → brand curtain, not bounce event.** Dissolve into the shelf wall turns compliance into the first brand beat; original had no gate, static had a dead modal. Expect fewer “close tab” exits at the door.

4. **Hero Ken-Burns on the real disposable wall** sells the shop in 3 seconds — the roommate demo moment. Original stocked emoji blocks; static had a still; motion makes the wall feel alive without video weight.

5. **818 scrub + stamp claim** frames the liquor flagship like a product launch (“Best 818 price in Reno”) then CTAs to *Check today’s price* — converts curiosity into a call, which is how this shop actually closes.

6. **Smirnoff mixer tips as three beats** keep liquor content sticky (content already on both sites) instead of a skim-past card — more time on `#deals` = more Midtown/McCarran intent.

7. **Locations as twin sliding doors** make “two shops, open late” tangible; static had the same data, motion makes Midtown vs McCarran feel like a choice you act on (Directions / Call).

8. **Glass showcase** is the Prism-adjacent desire beat without ecommerce risk: tilt the case photo → Call Midtown / See it Midtown. Differentiates vs every other Reno smoke site that never shows the case under lights.

9. **VIP card flip** rewards the Inner Circle submit with a loyalty-card moment — increases likelihood they *show the phone at the counter* (the actual conversion for this model).

10. **Lenis + ScrollTrigger (tuned, reduced-motion safe)** makes anchors and section rhythm feel intentional; original janky builder scroll and static’s light Lenis+IO don’t hold attention through a dual catalog.

11. **Marquee velocity tied to scroll** makes the vice ticker feel expensive, not carnival — reinforces Smoke + Liquor breadth without adding nav noise.

12. **Same CTAs, same copy, absolute CDN images** — A/B is apples-to-apples on message; the only variable is experience quality. Deploy HTML alone to Vercel and photos still load from `lavi-smoke-liquor.vercel.app/img/…`.
